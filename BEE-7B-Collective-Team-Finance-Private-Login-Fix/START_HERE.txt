BEE-7B Collective Team Finance - updated private login

1. On Netlify, open your existing site > Deploys > drag the SITE folder from this ZIP to the manual deploy area. Do not upload the whole ZIP.
2. Keep your existing Firebase project, Authentication users, Firestore data and previously published firestore.rules. No database reset needed.
3. Open the website in a private/incognito browser window: you should see Sign in / Create member account BEFORE any finance records.
4. Sign in as Owner: you should land on Admin dashboard with Transactions, Approvals, Members, Groups and Projects tabs.
5. Test editing a project name (and restore it), then register a separate test member and approve them.
6. An Owner can grant admin to an approved member from Admin > Approvals > Manage. Each admin has their own password.
7. Do not enter real financial records until login, permissions, edits, and cross-device sync are tested.

Note: Email/password login has no required email confirmation. Password-reset emails are optional and require Firebase email delivery.
